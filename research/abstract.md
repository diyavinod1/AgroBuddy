# Abstract

AgroBuddy is an AI-powered farmer assistance system delivered through Telegram. It combines computer vision, conversational AI, speech interfaces, multilingual interaction, and cloud persistence to provide accessible crop-health guidance to farmers. The system accepts crop images, predicts visible disease symptoms, produces farmer-friendly remedies, recommends fertilizer practices, stores user history, and supports English, Hindi, Tamil, Malayalam, and Kannada.

The project focuses on practical accessibility. Telegram removes the need for a custom mobile app, voice support reduces literacy barriers, multilingual responses support local adoption, and Grad-CAM explainability helps communicate which image regions influenced disease predictions.
